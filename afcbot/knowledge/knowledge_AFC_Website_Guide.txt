=== AFC WEBSITE GUIDE: HOW THE SITE WORKS NOW ===
Accurate as of 4 October 2026. Written from the live site and its code.
Website: https://africanfreefirecommunity.com
This guide replaces the older How-To Guide, Current Systems Guide and Support Guide. Where anything
older disagrees with this guide, this guide is right.

NOTES FOR THE ASSISTANT
- Never invent numbers (player counts, prize totals, tournament counts). If asked, say the live
  numbers are on the home page.
- Never promise an outcome only staff can decide: a registration approval, a refund, a ban appeal,
  a UID dispute, a tier change, a ticket reply time. Send the person to Support (section 15).
- Dates that change (transfer windows, events) are shown on the site itself. Say where to look.
- The site is in English, French and Portuguese. Answer in the language the person writes in.

--- 1. WHAT AFC IS ---
The African Free Fire Community (AFC) is a competitive Garena Free Fire platform for players across
Africa. On the site you can make a player profile, build and run a team, register for tournaments
and scrims, follow standings and the seasonal rankings, find a team or players on the Player
Market, vote in community polls and awards, and buy Free Fire diamonds in the shop.

Main pages (the menu):
- Home: /home

- Rules (the AFC Handbook): /rules

- Awards: /awards

--- 2. CREATING AN ACCOUNT ---
Go to /create-account (or "Join Now" at the top right).
You enter: full name, in-game name, email, password and confirm password, and tick that you agree
to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- Password: at least 8 characters with at least one number, one lowercase letter, one uppercase
  letter and one special character.
- WhatsApp number (optional): add it with your country code. It lets you recover your account by
  WhatsApp if you ever lose your email. One WhatsApp number can only belong to one account.
- Referral code (optional): if a friend or a stream gave you a code, enter it. An invite link fills
  it in for you.
After registering, confirm your email with the code we send (check spam). If it never arrives, the
email may be mistyped: the confirmation page lets you change it.
You can also sign up or sign in with Google, Discord or v-ent.co instead of a password.
Account creation is free.

First-time setup (you can skip any step and finish later from your profile):
1. Esports image: a clear, real photo of you (a bust shot). Organizers use it in event graphics.
2. Free Fire UID: the number from your Free Fire profile. Digits only.
3. Profile picture: your avatar on the site.

--- 3. SIGNING IN AND GETTING BACK IN ---
Log in at /login with your in-game name, your UID, or your email, plus your password. Or use
"Continue with Google", "Continue with Discord" or "Continue with v-ent.co".
- "Too many attempts from your connection" means wait a minute, then try again.
- "This account was deleted by its owner" means the account was deleted (section 4). Support can
  restore it.

Forgot your password: /forgot-password. Use the Email tab or the UID tab. We email a 6 digit code;
it expires after 10 minutes.

Lost access to your email too: use "Recover it with WhatsApp" (/recover-account). This only works
if you saved a WhatsApp number on your account. You enter your email, in-game name or UID, we send
a 6 digit code to that WhatsApp number (it works once and expires in 10 minutes), and then you
choose ONE fix: set a new password, or move the account to a new email address you can open.
Setting a new password signs you out everywhere. If you saved no WhatsApp number, contact Support.

Two-step sign-in (optional, on the Account and security page in your profile):
- After your password you also enter a 6 digit code, from your email or from an authenticator app
  (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password and similar). You can turn on both and pick each time.
- When you turn it on you get recovery codes. They are shown once: save them somewhere safe. Each
  works once and gets you in if you lose your email or phone.
- "Remember this device" skips the code on that browser for 30 days. Do not use it on a shared
  phone or computer.
- The same page lists remembered devices and other places you are signed in, and can sign them out.
- Accounts with admin or organizer powers are asked to turn it on.

--- 4. YOUR PROFILE AND ACCOUNT ---
Your profile (/profile) shows your details and stats, and holds these sections:
- Edit profile: name, email, in-game name, UID, country, language, pictures.
- In-game name: type it exactly as in Free Fire. Pasting is blocked so fancy unicode characters do
  not break match results.
- UID: digits only, and no other account may already use it. If someone else already holds your
  UID, open a Support ticket: staff sort out disputes, the assistant cannot.
- You CAN change your in-game name and UID yourself, EXCEPT while you are registered in an event
  that is upcoming or ongoing. They unlock once those events are over.
- Connected accounts: Discord, Google, v-ent.co. Connect them to sign in faster and to meet events
  that require them. If one is your only way to sign in, set a password before disconnecting it.
- Connected apps: other sites you signed into with your AFC account. You can cut any of them off.
- Discord: linking lets the AFC bot give you the right server and event roles.
- Referrals: your invite link and code (section 13).
- My reports: reports you filed and the admin answers.
- My support tickets: every message you sent AFC and every reply.
- QR code: a shareable QR for your player page (section 14).
- Delete my account: your account is hidden and you are signed out everywhere. Your in-game name,
  email, UID and WhatsApp number become free for a new account. Your match results and team history
  stay in AFC's records under a hidden name. A head admin can restore the account exactly as it was,
  as long as nobody has taken those details since. You cannot delete while you are in a team, own a
  team, organization, sponsor account or shop, hold a staff role, or are suspended or banned: settle
  that first.

--- 5. TEAMS ---
Browse teams at /teams. Each team page shows the overview, members, achievements and social links,
plus "I'm a fan" and "I'm a hater" buttons and a QR code.

Creating a team (/teams/create): team name, an optional tag (a short prefix like "AFC"), a logo,
and join settings:
- Open: any player can join instantly.
- By request: players ask to join and you approve or decline each one.
You can invite players by their AFC email or username; each invite must be accepted.
You can be in one team at a time.

Roles on a team:
- Players: Team Captain, Vice Captain, Member. Only players compete and can hold an in-game role
  (Rusher, Sniper, Support, Grenader), and a team can field at most 6.
- Staff: Coach, Manager, Analyst. Support only, never fielded as players, at most one of each.
- Moving someone between player and staff is only allowed while the transfer window is open.
- Who can manage the team: the owner always can. By default the owner and coaches manage the
  roster (captains cannot). The owner can change what each role may do under "Role permissions":
  inviting, handling join requests, editing the roster, removing members, registering for events,
  editing the team profile.

Other team settings:
- Team stats are private by default; the owner can make them public.
- Letter avatars: the Free Fire letter avatars the team can field. Members' own letters are added
  automatically; extra letters can be added by hand. Some events require a set of letters, and only
  the players you field for that event count.

Team country: worked out automatically from where the team's players are based. The country with
the most players wins. On a tie, the team owner's country decides. Some events only accept teams
from certain countries, which is why a team can be refused even if some members are from that
country. If the country looks wrong, contact Support.

--- 6. SEASONS AND TRANSFER WINDOWS ---
AFC runs in seasons of three months. Each season has a transfer window: a set period when roster
moves are free. Since 5 October 2026 the next season is created automatically when the current one
begins, starting the day after it ends, with its transfer window in its first two weeks. AFC staff
can still change any season's dates. The site shows them (the banner on the Rankings page and on team
pages), including the date the next window opens.
- SEASON 4 2026 runs from 11 September to 31 December 2026. Its transfer window ran from 11 to
  25 September 2026 and is now CLOSED. As of 5 October 2026 the next window opens on 1 January 2027,
  the first day of SEASON 1 2027 (its window runs 1 to 14 January 2027).
- The next window opens the day after the current season's last day, unless the next season on
  record says otherwise. After a season's last day, its lock no longer applies.

While the window is CLOSED:
- You CAN still create a team, join a team, send a join request, or accept an invite.
- You CANNOT leave a team, be removed from one, or disband one, until the next window opens.
- Before you join or create, the site warns you that you will not be able to leave, and tells you
  the date you will be free to leave (or that no date is set yet).
While the window is OPEN, players can move freely.

Transfers feed: every time a player joins or leaves a team that has competed, it is recorded
automatically. See it under News, Transfers (/news) and in "Who's moving in AFC" on the home page.
A move made while the window was closed is labelled "Window closed".

--- 7. TOURNAMENTS AND SCRIMS ---
/tournaments lists everything. Tabs: Tournaments, Scrims, Organizers. Filters: All, Upcoming,
Ongoing, Completed, plus organizer, date and sort. Scrims are practice events; they register the
same way as tournaments.

Each tournament carries a tier (Tier 1, Tier 2 or Tier 3, shown on its card). The tier changes how
many ranking points the event is worth (section 9).

Registering (open the event, then Register). Depending on the event you will see these steps:
1. Solo or Team: whichever the event does not allow is greyed out.
2. Your UID: required to compete, saved to your profile so you only enter it once.
3. Pick your players: for a team event, choose who plays, between the event's minimum and maximum
   squad size.
4. Rules: read them (download the full document if one is attached) and tick that you agree.
5. Sponsor task: some events ask each player to do a sponsor task (for example install an app) and
   enter an ID. Each ID must be unique; one someone else already used is refused.
6. Discord, ONLY when the event requires it: link your Discord account to AFC, then separately join
   the AFC Discord server and confirm. For a team, every selected player must do both. Many events
   do not require Discord at all.
7. Entry fee, when the event has one: paid online. Some events charge different amounts or nothing
   at all depending on your country (a squad uses its team country, a solo player their own).

Other things an event may require: a team logo, a player esports image and UID for every player,
letter avatars, a country, or an invite link for private events. Events also have a capacity; when
full, extra teams can go on a waitlist. AFC staff can excuse a team from some requirements (shown
as "Waived by AFC"); bans and payment can never be waived.

Check-in: some events ask you to check in during a window before the event. If you do not, you may
be moved to the waitlist. For teams the page shows how many teammates have checked in.

Times: event times are shown in YOUR timezone.

--- 8. LEADERBOARDS (PER EVENT) ---
/leaderboards shows each event's standings. Pick an event, then a stage, group and match.
"Overall Leaderboard" adds up every match in the group; pick a single match to see just that one.
Points are placement points plus points per kill, using the scoring the organizer set for that event.

--- 9. RANKINGS AND TIERS ---
/rankings has two tabs.
- Rankings: a live ladder across all events, ordered by score, for teams and for players. It
  rebuilds as results come in and resets on the 1st of each month. You can pick a season, month and
  country.
- Tiers: each team's grade for the whole season, regraded each quarter. Only teams are tiered.

The score combines tournament and scrim results: kills and placements (compressed so one huge event
cannot run away with it), bigger tournaments worth more, plus bonuses for wins and finals. Tap any
row for its full breakdown. The "How it works" button on the page explains it in detail.

Current values (AFC can change them; the page is always right):
- Tournament tier multipliers: Tier 1 2x, Tier 2 1.5x, Tier 3 1x. Win bonus: 30, 20 or 12 points.
- Team tiers: Tier 1 at 150+ points, Tier 2 at 90+, Tier 3 at 40+, everyone else Tier 4 (Entry).
- A team must play at least 2 tournaments in the quarter to rank above Tier 4. The "X/2
  tournaments" badge shows how far along a team is.
AFC can add more tiers in future; the Tiers tab shows whatever tiers exist.
A season's rankings and tiers only appear once AFC publishes them; until then the page says
"not published yet" and shows the latest published season.
Ghost teams: AFC records results for teams not yet on the site as placeholder "ghost" teams. The
real team can claim one to inherit its history and ranking.

--- 10. PLAYER MARKET ---
/player-markets is where teams find players and players find teams.
- Two post types: a team recruitment post (a team looking for players) or a player available post
  (a player looking for a team). You can have only ONE active post at a time. Close it or let it
  expire before making another.
- Posts can include up to 3 profile screenshots and one gameplay video.
- Applying to a team keeps your Discord and UID hidden until the team invites you to a trial.
- Trials: a private chat opens between the team and the player. You can be in at most 2 active
  trials at once. The team can extend a trial, recruit the player, or end it.
- Reports on the market go privately to moderators and need a screenshot as proof. A false or joke
  report can get the reporter banned from the market.

--- 11. NEWS, RULES, GLOSSARY ---
- News & Updates (/news): General News, Tournament Updates, Education Updates, Banned Player/Team
  Updates, and Transfers.
- Rules (/rules): the AFC Handbook, searchable. AFC admins have the final say on interpreting rules.
- Glossary (/glossary): plain explanations of esports and Free Fire terms.

--- 12. AWARDS, POLLS, FANTASY ---
- Awards (/awards): award seasons go through stages: nominees announced, voting open, votes being
  counted, winners announced. You can ask to be told when voting opens and when winners land. The
  Hall of fame lists past winners.
- Polls (/polls): community polls and award ballots. Sign in to vote. Each poll shows who can vote
  in it and whether you qualify. Some polls are anonymous (nobody, including admins, can see your
  answer); some let you change your answer until they close, others take your first answer as final.
  Results with fewer than 5 answers stay hidden. Team polls: the team's answer is the most common
  one among its players; on a tie, or if needed, the captain decides.
- Fantasy (/fantasy): NOT live yet. AFC is gauging interest: tick the box on the page if you would
  play it.

--- 13. REFERRALS ---
When AFC runs a referral program, your invite link and code are on your profile under Referrals,
with a QR code and WhatsApp share button. A referral only counts when the new player does what the
program asks (for example confirm their account, join or create a team, register for an event, or
make a first shop order). Prizes can be shop items, shop coupons or cash, at milestones or for the
top referrers. Rules: one referral per account, only brand new accounts count, and you cannot use
your own code. If no program is running, the Referrals section says so.

--- 14. QR CODES, NOTIFICATIONS, LANGUAGE ---
- QR codes: events, teams, player profiles and news articles have a "QR code" button. Download a
  plain PNG or SVG, a share card (1200 x 630, right for WhatsApp, X and Facebook), or print an A4
  poster. Whoever scans it lands on that page. The owner sees how many times theirs was scanned.
- Notifications: the bell shows updates about your tournaments, teams and orders. Many have a
  "Take me there" button that opens the right page.
- Language: English, French or Portuguese, set in your profile. Dates and times show in your own
  timezone.
- Feedback: the "Send feedback" button in the footer goes straight to the AFC team, signed in or not.

--- 15. GETTING HELP (SUPPORT) ---
- The Help button, bottom right of every page of the website (since 4 October 2026): the AFC
  assistant answers questions about the site straight away, signed in or not. Signed in, it can
  also check the person's own team, transfer window, registrations, bans and tickets. Its "Talk to
  a person" opens a Support ticket with the chat attached, so the person does not explain twice.
  It has a daily allowance of questions; the panel shows how many are left today.
- Support page (/support): write to AFC with the contact form. You get a ticket number by email (it
  looks like AFC-3F9A21), and every reply arrives by email too. You can attach pictures, video or
  documents.
- Signed in, "My tickets" on the same page lists every conversation with its status: "Waiting on
  AFC", "AFC replied, over to you", "Resolved" or "Closed". The menu shows a count when a ticket is
  waiting on you. The link in the email also lets you read the ticket and add to it.
- When a person has Discord connected, support replies can also arrive as a Discord message from
  the AFC bot.
- Report a player or team: the "Report player" / "Report team" button on their page. Reasons:
  cheating or hacking, toxic or abusive behaviour, harassment, impersonation, scam or fraud, other.
  Reports are private and go to AFC admins. A screenshot helps. False reports can get the reporter
  sanctioned. Your reports and the admin answers are under My reports in your profile.
- AFC Discord server: https://discord.gg/african-freefire-community-afc-920726990607237160
- Email: info@africanfreefirecommunity.com
- Social: X/Twitter @afcdatabase (https://twitter.com/afcdatabase), Instagram

Send people to Support for: UID or in-game name disputes, restoring a deleted account, a wrong team
country, ban or suspension appeals, payment problems, missing diamonds codes after 24 hours, and
anything about a specific registration being accepted or refused.

--- 16. SHOP AND DIAMONDS ---
/shop sells Free Fire diamonds and other items; My Orders (/orders) lists your orders.
- Pay with Paystack in Naira (card, bank transfer, USSD or mobile money), or by international card
  (shown and charged in your local currency).
- A 7.5% tax is added to the subtotal, after any coupon. Promo codes apply to the item they are
  added to (percentage or fixed amount off).
- Diamonds: after payment, a redemption code is emailed to you, usually within 5 to 10 minutes and
  in rare busy periods up to 24 hours. Redeem it on the official Free Fire site; it is NOT added to
  your game account automatically, and you do not enter a game ID at checkout.
- Order status: "Pending Verification" means the payment confirmation has not arrived yet; the site
  rechecks every 30 seconds and there is a Retry Now button. "Paid" means the code was sent to your
  registered email.
- Refunds are not available. For a payment problem (for example you were debited and nothing
  arrived), contact Support with your order.
- Some products are physical and ship to certain countries; the product shows where it ships.

--- 17. ORGANIZERS, PARTNERS, SPONSORS ---
- Organizations (/organizations): esports organizations and event organizers on AFC. Approved
  organizers host their own tournaments and scrims from an Organizer Dashboard.
- Become a partner (footer): apply to partner with AFC. The Partner API (footer) lets approved
  partners use AFC data and "Sign in with AFC" on their own sites.
- Sponsors can support events; a sponsor may set a task for players in an event (section 7).
- Brand kit (footer): AFC logos and brand assets.
